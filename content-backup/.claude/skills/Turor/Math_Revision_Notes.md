# Math Revision Notes

## Geometry
- **Circle Properties & Theorems**
    - Perpendicular bisector of chord passes through center
    - Equal chords are equidistant from center
    - Tangent is perpendicular to radius
    - Tangents from an external point are equal in length
    - Angle at center is twice the angle at circumference
    - Angle in a semicircle is a right angle (90°)
    - Angles subtended by the same arc are equal (Angles in the same segment)
    - Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral sum to 180°
    - Alternate Segment Theorem
    - Isosceles triangles (formed by radii)
    - Parts of a circle (Sector, Segment, Chord, etc.)
- **Angles in Parallel Lines**
    - Alternate angles are equal (Z-shape)
    - Corresponding angles are equal (F-shape)
    - Co-interior angles sum to 180° (C-shape)
- **Geometric Transformations**
    - Translation
    - Rotation
    - Reflection

## Algebraic Fractions
- **Addition & Subtraction**
    - Find common denominator
    - Convert to equivalent fractions
    - Add/Subtract numerators
    - Simplify fraction (lowest terms)
- **Multiplication**
    - Factorise first
    - Cancel common factors
    - Multiply numerators and denominators
- **Division**
    - Invert the second fraction (Reciprocal)
    - Change division sign to multiplication

## Probability
- Basic Formula (Number of successful outcomes / Total number of outcomes)
- Relative Frequency / Experimental Probability
- Expected Frequency
- **Event Relationships**
    - Mutually Exclusive Events: $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B)$
    - Independent Events: $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$
- **Dependent Events**
    - Tree Diagrams
    - Sampling without replacement

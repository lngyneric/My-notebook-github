# Term 1 Study Revision

> [!ABSTRACT] Overview
> Comprehensive revision notes for **Term 1**, covering the transition from the **Middle Ages** to the **Renaissance**, including key political, social, and cultural shifts.

## Unit 1: The Middle Ages

> [!INFO] Early Middle Ages & The Fall of Rome
> *   **Sources**: Distinction between **Primary** (original artifacts) & **Secondary** (analysis) sources.
> *   **Preservation**: Role of **Manuscripts & Monasteries** in keeping knowledge alive.
> *   **Fall of Western Roman Empire (476 AD)**: Marked the beginning of the Middle Ages.
> *   **Byzantine Empire**: The Eastern Roman Empire centered in **Constantinople**, lasting until the **Ottoman Conquest of 1453**.

### Key Social & Political Structures

> [!NOTE] Feudalism
> A social hierarchy based on land and labor exchange:
> 1.  **King** (Grants land)
> 2.  **Lords/Nobles** (Manage land, provide soldiers)
> 3.  **Knights** (Military service, Code of **Chivalry**)
> 4.  **Peasants/Serfs** (Work the land)

*   **Catholic Church**:
    *   Dominant institution in Europe.
    *   **Hierarchy**: Pope $\to$ Cardinals $\to$ Bishops $\to$ Priests.
    *   Language: **Latin** was the language of the Church and learning.

### Religious Movements & Conflicts
*   **Islamic Caliphates**:
    *   Rise of **Islam** & teachings of **Muhammad**.
    *   **Arab Expansion**: Spread of culture, science, and religion.
*   **Crusades**:
    *   Religious wars for control of the **Holy Land**.
    *   **Impact**: Facilitated the exchange of **Spices**, **Science**, and **Technology** between East and West.

## Unit 2: The Renaissance

> [!TIP] Core Concepts: Humanism
> The shift from religious-centric views to focusing on human potential and achievements.
> *   **Individualism**: Value of the individual.
> *   **Secularism**: Focus on worldly rather than spiritual matters.
> *   **Rationalism**: Use of reason and logic.

### Art & Architecture
*   **Key Figures**: **Leonardo da Vinci** (e.g., *"Mona Lisa"*, 1503-1519).
*   **Techniques**:
    *   **Perspective** (Depth)
    *   **Fresco** (Wall painting)
    *   **Emotional Expression** (Realism)
*   **Architecture**:
    *   Revival of **Classical Columns** (Greek/Roman style).
    *   Emphasis on **Symmetry & Proportion**.
    *   Use of **Domes & Arches** (e.g., Brunelleschi's Dome).

### The Spread of Ideas & Science
*   **Gutenberg Printing Press (1440s)**:
    *   Revolutionized communication.
    *   Led to a massive **increase in literacy** and rapid spread of ideas.
*   **Northern Renaissance**: Known for detailed **Oil Painting**.

> [!EXAMPLE] Scientific Revolution (1500s-1600s)
> *   **Heliocentrism vs. Geocentrism**: Shift from Earth-centered to Sun-centered universe (Copernicus, Galileo).
> *   **Trial of Galileo (1633)**: Conflict between Science and the Church.
> *   **Isaac Newton**: Defined **Gravity** & **Laws of Motion**.

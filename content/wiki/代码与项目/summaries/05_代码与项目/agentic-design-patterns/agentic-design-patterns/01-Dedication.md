## Abstract
This is the dedication page of the *Agentic Design Patterns* book. The author dedicates the work to his four children, and expresses his core vision that the intelligent technologies (the main focus of the book) should be guided by wisdom and compassion to serve humanity and drive human progress.

## Key Points
1. **Dedication Recipients**: The author's four children: 2-year-old Bruno, sons Leonardo and Lorenzo, and daughter Aurora.
2. **Author's Sentiments**: The author takes pride in his elder children's growth and their efforts to build a better world, and always keeps in mind the future world that young Bruno will inherit.
3. **Core Aspiration**: While the book focuses on the methodology of building intelligent tools, the author hopes the younger generation will govern these powerful technologies with wisdom and compassion, to ensure technology serves humanity and creates a bright shared future for all.

## Evidence Snippets
### English Original
To my son, Bruno,

who at two years old, brought a new and brilliant light into my life. As I explore the systems that will define our tomorrow, it is the world you will inherit that is foremost in my thoughts.

To my sons, Leonardo and Lorenzo, and my daughter Aurora,

My heart is filled with pride for the women and men you have become and the wonderful world you are building.

This book is about how to build intelligent tools, but it is dedicated to the profound hope that your generation will guide them with wisdom and compassion. The future is incredibly bright, for you and for us all, if we learn to use these powerful technologies to serve humanity and help it progress.

With all my love.

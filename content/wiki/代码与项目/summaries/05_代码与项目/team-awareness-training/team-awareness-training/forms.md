# Team Awareness Training Forms
## Summary
This document outlines standardized anonymous pre-course and post-course forms for team awareness training programs, administered by the course teacher to collect baseline participant metrics, post-training feedback, and correlate paired pre/post participant data via a consistent anonymous identifier.

## Key Points
### General Form Guidelines
1. Forms are to be distributed by the course teacher before the start and after the completion of the training.
2. Any form tool may be used; Google Forms is a previously tested and utilized option.
3. All participants must be explicitly informed that form responses are fully anonymous.
4. A unique, participant-selected "secret handle" is used to link pre-course and post-course responses without compromising participant anonymity.

### Pre-course Form Fields
The pre-course form collects baseline participant data and enrollment context, with the following required fields:
- Immediate current stress level (1-10 rating scale)
- Average stress level over the prior 2 weeks (1-10 rating scale)
- Average focus level over the prior 2 weeks (1-10 rating scale)
- General well-being over the prior 2 weeks (1-10 rating scale)
- Current methods the participant uses to reduce stress
- Daily number of flow sessions experienced by the participant (0-5 rating scale)
- Participant's personal goal for enrolling in the course
- Secret handle (to be saved by the participant for use in the post-course form)

### Post-course Form Fields
The post-course form collects post-training metrics, feedback, and outcome assessment, with the following required fields:
- Immediate current stress level (1-10 rating scale)
- Average stress level over the prior 2 weeks (1-10 rating scale)
- Average focus level over the prior 2 weeks (1-10 rating scale)
- General well-being over the prior 2 weeks (1-10 rating scale)
- Daily number of flow sessions experienced by the participant (0-5 rating scale)
- Teacher performance rating (1-10 rating scale)
- Qualitative feedback for the course teacher
- Overall course rating (1-10 rating scale)
- Qualitative feedback on course content, structure, or delivery
- Course ROI (percentage rating of perceived value relative to time invested in the course)
- Open field for additional feedback
- Secret handle (matching the pre-course submission to enable paired data correlation)

Skill Description

Automated Skill: New Employee Onboarding and Training Program
自动化技能：新员工入职培训计划

This document outlines the structure, design, and workflow logic for an automated skill designed to manage the new employee onboarding and training process. The goal is to systematically guide new hires through initial onboarding, foundational skills, specialized knowledge, and practical application to ensure they meet role-specific competencies and company standards for formal employment.
本文档概述了自动化技能的结构、设计和工作流程逻辑，旨在管理新员工的入职和培训流程。目标是系统地引导新员工完成初步入职、基础技能、专业知识和实际应用，确保他们符合岗位特定能力和公司正式聘用标准。

Automated Skill: New Employee Onboarding and Training Program
自动化技能：新员工入职培训计划

Skill Name: New Employee Onboarding and Training Program Automation
技能名称： 新员工入职与培训项目自动化

Purpose: To systematically guide new employees through their initial onboarding, foundational skills training, specialized knowledge acquisition, and on-site practical application, ensuring they meet role-specific competencies and company standards for formal employment. This automation manages the progression, assessments, and outcomes of the training journey.
目的： 系统地指导新员工完成初次入职培训、基础技能培训、专业知识获取及现场实践应用，确保他们符合岗位特定能力和公司正式就业标准。该自动化管理培训进程、评估和成果。

Workflow Logic Mapping
工作流程逻辑映射

The automation follows a structured, phased approach:
自动化采用结构化、分阶段的方法：

Phase 1: Initial Onboarding and Training Path Assignment
第一阶段：初始入职及培训路径分配

  1. Initiation: New employee officially reports for duty.
    启蒙仪式： 新员工正式报到。
  2. HR Onboarding Procedures:
    人力资源入职流程：
    • Collect mandatory personal data (name, ID, education, health certificate details, contact information) as per Employee Handbook Chapter 4, Section 6-1.
      根据《员工手册》第4章第6-1节，收集强制性的个人信息（姓名、身份证、教育背景、健康证明详情、联系方式）。
    • Confirm labor contract signing.
      确认劳动合同签署。
    • Provide access to the Employee Handbook and relevant company policies.
      提供员工手册和相关公司政策的访问权限。
    • Initiate social insurance transfer procedures.
      启动社会保险转移程序。
  3. Initial Managerial Orientation:
    初级管理导向：
    • Schedule and facilitate a meeting with the direct superior to discuss MBO objectives, responsibilities, and department operational procedures.
      安排并协调与直接上级的会议，讨论 MBO 的目标、职责及部门运营程序。
    • Facilitate a session on CRM system usage, meeting protocols, and reporting standards.
      主持一场关于 CRM 系统使用、符合协议和报告标准的会议。
    • Ensure acknowledgement of HR and Supervisor guidance.
      确保确认人力资源和主管的指导。
  4. Decision Point - Employee Level Classification:
    决策点 - 员工级别分类：

    Condition: Check Employee_Role for "Manager level or above" (是否是主管以上的一线员工？).
    条件：检查 Employee_Role 是否“经理级或以上”（ 是否是主管以上的一线员工？）。

    If TRUE: Training path set to include "Supervisor Development Training" first.
    如果属实： 培训路径将首先包括“主管发展培训”。

    If FALSE: Training path proceeds directly to "General Skills Training".
    如果是错误： 培训路径直接进入“通用技能培训”。

Phase 2: Core Skills Development (General & Supervisor)
第二阶段：核心技能发展（综合与主管）

For Manager level or above (Pre-requisite: Supervisor Development Training - 第二阶段 小灶培训):
对于管理层及以上（先决条件：主管发展培训 - 第二阶段 小灶培训 ）：

  1. Supervisor Specific Modules: Training on Channel Management, Market Promotion, Digital Management, Customer Management, Team Management, and Regulation & Problem Solving.
    导师专属模块： 渠道管理、市场推广、数字管理、客户管理、团队管理以及监管与问题解决等培训。
  2. Upon completion, proceed to "General Skills Training".
    完成后，进入“通用技能培训”。

For All Employees (General Skills Training - 阶段1 通用技能培训):
对所有员工（通用技能培训阶段 1 通用技能培训 ）：

  1. Guidance on Superior/Customer Accounts: Training on professional interaction with superiors and fundamental customer account management.
    高级/客户账户指南： 培训内容包括与上级的专业互动及基础客户账户管理。
  2. Corporate Culture Learning: Modules on company values, ethics, and corporate culture.
    企业文化学习： 关于公司价值观、伦理和企业文化的模块。
  3. Communication Skills Assessment: Automated communication skills exam (通讯归考).
    沟通能力评估： 自动沟通技能考试（ 通讯归考 ）。
  4. Decision Point - Communication Exam Score:
    决策点 - 沟通考试分数：

    Condition: Communication_Exam_Score >= 80 points (≥80分?).
    条件：Communication_Exam_Score>= 80 分 （≥80 分？）。

    If TRUE: Proceed to Phase 3: Specialized Training.
    如果属实： 进入第三阶段：专业培训。

    If FALSE: Trigger "Counseling & Enhancement" (辅导补强) sub-process. Provide remedial materials and re-assessment. Persistent failure escalates to HR for performance review.
    如果是错误： 触发“ 辅导补强 ”子流程。提供补救材料并重新评估。持续失败会升级到人力资源部门进行绩效评估。

  5. End of Core Skills Development Phase.
    核心技能发展阶段结束。

Phase 3: Specialized Knowledge Acquisition (阶段2 专业知识培训)
第三阶段：专业知识培训（ 第二阶段 专业知识培训 ）

  1. Specialized Guidance: Role-specific guidance from superiors on advanced customer account management or specific product portfolios.
    专业指导： 上级提供关于高级客户账户管理或特定产品组合的岗位指导。
  2. Product Information Training: Detailed training on specific product features, technical specifications, and market positioning (产品信息解读).
    产品信息培训： 详细培训内容涵盖具体产品特性、技术规格和市场定位（ 产品信息解读 ）。
  3. Specialized Training Assessment: Submit required training deliverables or take a specialized knowledge exam (提交培训/考试).
    专业培训评估： 提交所需的培训成果或参加专业知识考试（ 提交培训/考试 ）。
  4. Decision Point - Specialized Training Score:
    决策点 - 专业培训评分：

    Condition: Specialized_Training_Assessment_Score >= 80 points (≥80分?).
    条件： Specialized_Training_Assessment_Score >= 80 分 （≥80 分？）。

    If TRUE: Record "Notification Completed" (通知完成) and proceed to Phase 4: On-site Practice.
    如果属实： 记录“通知完成”（ 通知完成 ），进入第四阶段：现场练习。

    If FALSE: Trigger "Counseling & Enhancement" (辅导补强) sub-process. Provide remedial materials and re-assessment. Persistent failure escalates to HR for performance review.
    如果是错误： 触发“ 辅导补强 ”子流程。提供补救材料并重新评估。持续失败会升级到人力资源部门进行绩效评估。

  5. End of Specialized Knowledge Acquisition Phase.
    专业知识获取阶段结束。

Phase 4: On-site Practical Application (阶段3 岗位实操)
第四阶段：现场实践应用（ 第三阶段 岗位实 ）

  1. Task Assignment: Assign specific practical tasks or projects relevant to the employee's role (安排任务).
    任务分配： 分配与员工角色相关的具体实际任务或项目（ 安排任务 ）。
  2. On-site Practice Execution: Carry out assigned tasks in a real-world working environment (实操作).
    现场实践执行： 在真实工作环境中执行分配的任务（ 实作 ）。
  3. On-site Guidance: Receive real-time coaching and feedback from a mentor or supervisor (现场指导).
    现场指导： 获得导师或主管的实时指导和反馈（ 现场指导 ）。
  4. Service Report Submission: Submit comprehensive reports detailing practical work and achievements (提交服务报告).
    服务报告提交： 提交详尽的报告，详细说明实际工作和成就（ 提交服务报告 ）。
  5. Decision Point - Final Performance Evaluation:
    决策点 - 最终绩效评估：

    Condition: Overall On_site_Evaluation_Result is "Passed" (考核通过?).
    状态：总体 On_site_Evaluation_Result“通过”（ 考核通过？）。

    If TRUE: Initiate "Formal Employment" (正式录用) procedures.
    如果属实： 启动“正式录用 ”程序。

    If FALSE: Initiate "Termination" procedures (终止) as per Employee Handbook (e.g., Chapter 9, Section 9-2; Chapter 10, Section 10-1).
    如果是错误： 根据员工手册启动“终止”程序（例如第 9 章第 9-2 节;第 10 章，第 10-1 节）。

Alternative/Specific Track: New Employee Training Manager Track (第三阶段 新员工训练营)
替代/特定方向：新员工培训经理方向（ 第三阶段 新员工训练营 ）

Initial training path for new employees designated as "Training Managers".
新员工被指定为“培训经理”的初始培训路径。

  1. Self-study and Preliminary Exam: Complete self-study materials and pass an initial exam.
    自学与初试： 完成自学材料并通过初试。
  2. Training Camp: Attend a mandatory 3-day training camp.
    训练营： 参加为期三天的强制训练营。
  3. Evaluation Exam and Feedback: Undergo a final evaluation exam and receive performance feedback.
    评估考试与反馈： 参加最终评估考试并获得绩效反馈。
  4. Decision Point - Evaluation Score:
    决策点 - 评估分数：

    Condition: Evaluation_Exam_Score >= 80 points (≥80分?).
    条件：Evaluation_Exam_Score>= 80 分 （≥80 分？）。

    If TRUE: Successfully complete this track and proceed to Phase 3: Specialized Training (of the main workflow).
    如果属实： 成功完成此轨道，进入第三阶段：专业培训（主工作流程）。

    If FALSE: Trigger "Counseling & Enhancement" (辅导补强) sub-process. Persistent failure escalates to HR for review.
    如果是错误： 触发“ 辅导补强 ”子流程。持续失败会升级到人力资源部门进行审核。

Trigger Conditions
触发条件

  • Employee Onboarding Event: New employee's official start date or "Report for Duty" event in the HR system.
    员工入职活动： 新员工的正式入职日期或人力资源系统中的“报到”事件。
  • Training Module Completion: Successful completion (including passing assessments) of a preceding training module.
    培训模块完成： 成功完成（包括通过评估）之前的培训模块。
  • Assessment Failure: Employee's score falling below the predefined threshold (e.g., 80 points) on a required assessment.
    评估失败： 员工在强制评估中得分低于预定门槛（例如 80 分）。
  • On-site Evaluation Outcome: Recording of a "Pass" or "Fail" result for the final on-site practice evaluation.
    现场评估结果： 记录“通过”或“不及格”的最终现场实习评估结果。

输入/输出需求

Error Handling Logic
错误处理逻辑

  • Incomplete/Invalid Input Data: If mandatory documents/data are missing or invalid (e.g., missing health certificate as per 6-1-2, false information as per 10-1), the system pauses the step, notifies the employee and HR of the discrepancy, and awaits correction.
    输入数据不完整/无效： 如果强制性文件/数据缺失或无效（例如，6-1-2 规定的健康证明缺失，10-1 规定的虚假信息），系统会暂停该步骤，通知员工和人力资源部门差异，并等待更正。
  • Persistent Training/Assessment Failure: If an employee fails an assessment (score < 80) after a predefined number of "Counseling & Enhancement" attempts (e.g., two re-attempts), the system escalates the case to HR and the direct superior for formal performance review, potentially leading to position adjustment or termination (Chapter 9, Section 9-2).
    持续的培训/评估失败： 如果员工在预定次数的“咨询与提升”尝试（例如两次重试）后未通过评估（得分 3C 80），系统会将案件升级至人力资源部门及直接上级进行正式绩效评估，可能导致职位调整或解雇（第 9 章，第 9-2 节）。
  • Non-compliance with Policy: If a conflict of interest or policy violation is identified (e.g., dual employment as per 6-3 or 10-5), the system flags the employee, halts progression, and triggers disciplinary action based on Chapter 10, Sections 40-1 or 40-2, and Chapter 42.
    不遵守政策： 如果发现利益冲突或政策违规（例如，根据 6-3 或 10-5 条款的双重工作），系统会标记该员工，停止晋升，并根据第 10 章第 40-1 或 40-2 条及第 42 章触发纪律处分。
  • Managerial Non-Response/Delay: If approvals or feedback from managers are not received within specified SLAs, automated reminders are sent, and escalation to higher management or HR occurs.
    管理层的不回应/拖延： 如果经理的批准或反馈未在指定 SLA 内收到，会自动发送提醒，并向上级管理或人力资源部门升级。
  • System Malfunction/Unavailable Resources: If a required system or resource is unavailable, the system logs the error, notifies IT support, pauses the affected step, and provides a manual workaround option if feasible.
    系统故障/资源不可用： 如果需要的系统或资源不可用，系统会记录错误，通知 IT 支持，暂停受影响的步骤，并在可行的情况下提供手动变通方案。
  • Unexpected Evaluation Outcome (Failure): Upon a "Failed" result in the final "On-site Practice Evaluation", the system automatically triggers "Termination" procedures, adhering to legal and internal policy guidelines (Chapter 11).
    意外评估结果（失败）： 在最终的“现场实践评估”中出现“失败”结果后，系统会自动触发“终止”程序，遵守法律和内部政策指南（第 11 章）。
